Marco finished a 1-month research secondment in our lab under the supervision of Federico Olivieri.
Supported by my SMASH MSCA fellowship, we aimed to develop protein binders specific for an unstructured epitope in the CDKL5 biomarker, critical for brain development. Four binders were successfully designed from 24 tested candidates.
